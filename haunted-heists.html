The pitch

Greed is the difficulty setting.

Paranormal Estates: Haunted Robberies is a cooperative horror game about thieves, not investigators. Every stolen item raises the stakes, and the safest exit is always one valuable away from being a terrible idea.

01

Case the Property

Arrive at the vehicle, choose equipment, and identify a way inside.

02

Break In

Hack or lockpick entry points while noise and time begin working against the crew.

03

Steal Everything

Search rooms, manage limited inventory space, and deposit secured loot in the van.

04

Survive the House

Respond to escalating events, hunts, panic, and a ghost that learns where players have been.

05

Extract

Escape with the crew—or leave with fewer people and one extremely expensive vase.

Gameplay systems

The house reacts to what players do.

The horror is systemic: player behavior feeds pressure, pressure shapes events, and events change how the crew moves through the property.

D

Adaptive Director

A server-authoritative Director scores eligible players using panic, threat pressure, recent targeting, and house state. It paces scares and hunts while protecting against repetitive targeting and overlapping haunts.

  • Weighted target selection
  • Adaptive cooldowns and recovery windows
  • Global and victim-specific event routing
AI

Ghost State Machine

Ghosts roam, investigate noise, chase on confirmed sight, search last-known locations, interact with doors, and return to whole-house roaming when the trail goes cold.

P

Panic & House Activity

Player panic drives vulnerability while House Activity preserves the consequences of theft. The crew can recover; the house does not forget.

L

Loot & Inventory

Items have size, weight, hidden value, world interaction, replicated pickup/drop behavior, van storage, and a final extraction reveal.

E

Environmental Horror

Door slams, mirror apparitions, screams, light chases, breaker failures, and house audio are routed through shared scare rules instead of isolated jump-scare scripts.

UI

Minimal Information

Loot value and panic stay hidden during the robbery. Players read the world, their teammates, and sound—not a dashboard full of certainty.

Technical architecture

Server authority without flattening the scares.

The server decides what is true; presentation is routed only to the players who should experience it.

Challenge

Make fear fair in multiplayer

Some scares must be shared while others only work if the intended victim sees them.

SolutionServer-selected targets with explicit global, area, and victim-local presentation paths.

Challenge

Keep tension from becoming exhaustion

Constant hunts turn horror into a predictable hide-and-seek loop.

SolutionPressure-aware cooldowns, recovery states, recent-target penalties, and one active haunt at a time.

Challenge

Let ghosts feel intelligent

Instant omniscience is frustrating; instant amnesia is goofy.

SolutionLOS-gated pursuit, last-known-position search, noise investigation, and whole-house roaming after a failed search.

Design pillars

What Paranormal Estates: Haunted Robberies protects.

Horror before chores

Pressure comes from decisions and uncertainty, not busywork disguised as difficulty.

Greed creates stories

The funniest disasters begin when the crew agrees to take just one more item.

Readable, not predictable

Players can learn the rules without knowing exactly when the house will use them.

Co-op chaos with consequences

Teammates can save the mission, endanger everyone, or become the reason a lamp was apparently worth dying for.

Equipment, Heist Tools & Economy Update

October 3, 2026

The robbery now starts before you reach the house. This update expands the full equipment loop—from buying tools, to loading the van, to surviving long enough to use them.

Ω — THE HEIST GETS AN ECONOMY

Three milestones. One connected equipment loop.

Across v0.4.5, v0.5.0, and v0.5.5, Haunted Robberies gained equipment preparation, usable heist tools, BossMan's persistent shop economy, equipment ownership and loss, and vehicle progression.

The new Paranormal Estates launcher changes distribution too: install once, then let the launcher handle game installation and future updates.

Φ rebuilt what you steal. Ω changes what you bring, what you risk, and what comes home with you.

Equipment UI & Loadouts

  • Added the BossMan Shop, including money, shop upgrades, item prices, ownership limits, and shop-level requirements.
  • Added a shared pre-heist equipment pool. Bring equipment you own into the van before the job, then collect it during the heist.
  • Added Add All and Remove All controls for faster loadout setup.
  • Overhauled the Van Inventory with item icons, quantities, vehicle cargo capacity, item descriptions, hover details, and a scrollable item list.
  • Added clearer house requirements—larger estates now tell you when your crew needs a larger vehicle before you can select them.

Heist Tools

  • Added and expanded Flashlight, Headlamp, Night Vision, Glowsticks, battery packs, portable work lamps, lockpicks, electronic bypass tool, Crowbar, wire cutters, dolly, medicine, and noise makers.
  • Equipment pickups can now be configured for instant interaction.
  • Head-worn tools use their own equipment slot and stay equipped until dropped. R toggles worn equipment; T drops it.
  • Battery packs refill compatible tools and are consumed after use. Medicine and other consumables are destroyed after use.
  • Crowbars can force compatible doors quickly but loudly; wire cutters can permanently disable breaker wiring.
  • Lockpick upgrades provide safer ways to deal with locks, while the bypass tool handles electronic security.

Noise Makers & Ghost Distractions

  • Added basic noise makers that can be toggled on and off.
  • Added remote noise makers with a separate remote-control item and a short activation cooldown.
  • Ghosts can investigate active noise makers, turn them off, and may break them in the process.
  • Improved ghost focus so major threats and distractions are prioritized more intelligently.

Equipment Economy

  • Organized equipment around shop progression and availability tiers.
  • Added clearer equipment categories and removed irrelevant setup clutter from item data.
  • Protected essential default-owned equipment from permanent loss.
  • If a player dies, dropped gear remains available for surviving teammates during the heist.
  • Equipment loss is finalized only after extraction or game over.
  • Improved quantity limits and loadout balancing for multiplayer crews.

Other Improvements

  • Improved crouching visuals and multiplayer replication so players appear lower while hiding.
  • Improved breaker states, destroyed-breaker feedback, and break-in alarm behavior.
  • Added improved house-loot and cargo planning tools to support house/van progression.
  • Continued vehicle-tier gating so larger jobs require the right van.

Launcher & Updating

  • Introduced the PE:HR Launcher as the new unified way to install and update Haunted Robberies.
  • Retired the current website's separate Steam and EOS download choices in favor of one launcher flow.
  • Future builds can be delivered through the updater without requiring players to manually replace the full game package.
