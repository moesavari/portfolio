Soul Snatched: Ginger Edition

2D top-down village defense ARPG - soul-siphoning, morality, and wave-based survival.

Overview

Soul Snatched: Ginger Edition is a 2D top-down action RPG where a lone ginger defends villages from nightly zombie invasions. By day, you prepare defenses, upgrade gear, and manage relationships. By night, you survive waves, siphon souls, and walk the line between protector and monster.

Core Systems Implemented

  • Day/Night loop controlling village recovery, crafting, and zombie invasions.
  • Wave-based enemy spawning with difficulty scaling and multi-lane routing.
  • Morality and reputation system tied to soul-siphoning villagers.
  • Hair-as-magic visual indicator that tracks your soul power and sanity.
  • Vendor and shop UI with reputation-based inventory and item quality.
  • Equipment and stats system driving player power and survivability.
  • Event-driven audio and UI feedback for combat, waves, and upgrades.

My Role

Solo developer - responsible for all gameplay programming, systems design, UI implementation, and project architecture.
